# Kitchen Journal

A warm, organic, and artisanal theme designed for a pet recipe and ingredient-led brand, reading like a hand-written recipe notebook.

## Color Palette

- **Paper (Background)**: `#F3ECDC` - Warm kraft paper primary background
- **Paper Light (Surface)**: `#FAF6EC` - Card and surface background
- **Ink (Text)**: `#2E2418` - Roasted coffee text color, high contrast
- **Turmeric (Accent)**: `#C98A1F` - Golden yellow spice primary accent
- **Herb (Natural Accent)**: `#4B6B3A` - Trust and natural green for labels and eyebrows
- **Paprika (CTA)**: `#B8451E` - Bright earthy red-orange for calls to action
- **Cardboard (Borders)**: `#C9BB9C` - Soft neutral borders, dividers, and dotted rules

## Typography

- **Headers**: Fraunces (Display serif, weight 600-700 / italic 400)
- **Body Text**: Karla (Clean, highly readable sans-serif)
- **Data/transparency**: IBM Plex Mono (Technical mono-font for lists and numbers)

## Best Used For

Scooby's Kitchen main website, ingredient lists, recipe cards, and transparency disclosures.
