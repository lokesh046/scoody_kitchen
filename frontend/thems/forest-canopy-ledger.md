# Forest Canopy Ledger

A natural, grounded, and cozy theme designed for an organic pet brand, pairing Dark Roasted Coffee with Muted Sage and Forest Green.

## Color Palette

- **Paper (Background)**: `#F9F6F0` - Warm Cream primary background
- **Paper Light (Surface)**: `#FFFFFF` - Pure white card surfaces
- **Ink (Text)**: `#362820` - Dark Roasted Coffee text color
- **Turmeric (Accent)**: `#D09E6B` - Warm Ochre primary accent/titles
- **Herb (Natural Accent)**: `#8FA89B` - Muted Sage Green for success labels and active badges
- **Paprika (CTA)**: `#3F5E4D` - Forest Green for calls to action
- **Cardboard (Borders)**: `#EBE0D0` - Creamy Biscuit borders and dividers
- **Vibrant Gold (Logo Accent)**: `#FFDD43` - Golden logo highlight in the header

## Typography

- **Headers**: Outfit (Display sans-serif, weight 700-900)
- **Body Text**: Quicksand (Clean, friendly sans-serif)
- **Data/transparency**: IBM Plex Mono (Technical mono-font for lists and numbers)

## Best Used For

Scooby's Kitchen main website, fresh-prepared pet food catalogs, veterinary consultations logs, and administrative settings.
