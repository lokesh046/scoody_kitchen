# Onyx Gold Ledger

A rich, premium, and sophisticated theme designed for a high-end pet brand, pairing Onyx Black with Sand Gold and Deep Burgundy.

## Color Palette

- **Paper (Background)**: `#FFF9F2` - Warm milk cream primary background
- **Paper Light (Surface)**: `#FFFFFF` - Pure white card surfaces
- **Ink (Text)**: `#171614` - Onyx Black text color
- **Turmeric (Accent)**: `#C5A77A` - Sand Beige Gold primary accent/titles
- **Herb (Natural Accent)**: `#17352B` - Forest Green for success labels and active badges
- **Paprika (CTA)**: `#541E22` - Deep Burgundy for calls to action
- **Cardboard (Borders)**: `#EBE0D0` - Creamy Biscuit borders and dividers
- **Navy (Secondary Accent)**: `#17233D` - Midnight Navy secondary accent

## Typography

- **Headers**: Outfit (Display sans-serif, weight 700-900)
- **Body Text**: Quicksand (Clean, friendly sans-serif)
- **Data/transparency**: IBM Plex Mono (Technical mono-font for lists and numbers)

## Best Used For

Scooby's Kitchen main website, premium product grids, vet consults dashboard, and admin dashboards.
